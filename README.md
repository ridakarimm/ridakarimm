## Featured projects

**[Femicide in the United States](https://github.com/ridakarimm/femicide-analysis)**<br>
Analyzed 8,000 recorded femicide cases to find where rates are highest per capita and what sets multi-victim cases
apart. Built a pandas pipeline that de-identifies and cleans the data, mapped per-capita rates against 2020 Census
populations, and trained an XGBoost classifier with 5-fold cross-validation to rank the factors that matter most.

**Grant Intelligence for AnnieCannons**, 2nd place at the JPMorgan Chase Data for Good Hackathon<br>
Co-developed a grant prioritization model utilized by AnnieCannons' grant team to predict which grants they are most
likely to win and how much each could pay out, using logistic regression in scikit-learn tested on funders the model
had never seen. Also built an NLP tool that scores draft grant answers from 0 to 100 by comparing them with similar
past answers (TF-IDF similarity) across 1,693 Q&A pairs. Delivered as a Streamlit app.
